# CasePilot Clinician Questionnaire

**Audience:** School social workers, school counselors, school psychologists, and other school-based mental-health clinicians
**Estimated completion time:** 10–15 minutes

## Purpose

We are researching how school-based clinicians manage student-support history, follow-ups, meetings, interventions, and referrals. Your responses will help determine which workflow problems are most important to solve.

Please do not include student names, initials, dates of birth, student IDs, contact information, or any other information that could identify a student or family. Generalized examples are sufficient.
